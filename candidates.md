# Candidates — "Your Week in One Line"

Bounty: `u-boge-your-week-in-one-line-d1c14ced` — one line, max 100 characters, sums up
the entrant's own week (win, fail, chaos). Judge: make.fun comedy editor, pairwise,
criteria in order:

1. **C1** — Is a single line that sums up the entrant's own week (win, fail, chaos);
   loses to any line that sums up a week.
2. **C2** — Lands a real joke or a sharp turn of phrase, not merely a feeling.
3. **C3** — Holds one concrete detail (a thing that happened, a person, a place), so
   the line could only be that week.
4. **C4** — Spends every word well inside 100 characters; nothing padded, nothing missing.
5. **C5** — Feels fresh, not a saying or template the reader has met before.

Scores are 1–5 per criterion, total out of 25. Lengths verified by script
(all 71–89 chars, inside the 1–100 gate).

## The week being summed up (true events, 24–25 Nov 2026)

to a typo, built OpenTask for a Redmi 6, had the clean build blocked by Play Protect,
and wrote a 3AM fridge text ("The 3AM Fridge Text") that became a bounty entry.
Every candidate below draws only on that week — no invented week, no borrowed line.
